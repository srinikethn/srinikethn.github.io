About

Not just what I’ve done, but how I think. This is the throughline behind my lab work, robotics projects, and system design experiments.

I’ve always gravitated toward problems that refuse to stay in one discipline. In the lab, that looks like tightening the connection between experiment design and downstream analysis. In engineering, it means building systems where hardware, perception, and decision-making are architected together instead of patched on top of each other.

I like environments where I’m trusted with context, not just tickets. Give me the constraints, the messy data, and the long-term goal—and I’ll help design the interfaces, feedback loops, and technical architecture to get us there in a way that’s both rigorous and maintainable.

Over time, I’ve realized my core skill isn’t just “knowing tools”—it’s learning systems fast, spotting where structure is missing, and then building the scaffolding (protocols, code, or documentation) that lets a team move faster without losing rigor.
